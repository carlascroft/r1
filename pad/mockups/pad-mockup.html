tab-aware padThe thing ZAPAD structurally can't do. Keyed off the active tab rather than the frontmost app, so each generator gets its own face and the keys call your endpoints instead of pretending to be a keyboard. Cyan here only to show the pad travels — the family rule still decides.

What's drawn vs what's filledGrid lines are permanent and coloured; fill means state — pressed, held, occupied. Nothing appears or disappears, which keeps the pad readable as one object rather than a list.
